## Aviator Game Rules

Many different websites host Aviator because they know how popular it is. Don’t worry, though, the rules and mechanics stay identical no matter which platform you end up picking. This is what you’ll have to do:

<ul>
<li>Decide when you want to enter the round. Aviator is a short game: multiple people play it all the time. 10-20 seconds, and the round is over.</li>
<li>Once you enter the round and it starts, a plane will appear on your screen. It’ll be flying quickly, hoping to reach the highest sky level.</li> 
<li>The multiplier will also appear, and it’ll keep running just as quickly, accompanying the plane on its flight.</li> 
<li>The players will start withdrawing from the round. Some will do it at the beginning; others will wait a bit longer.</li> 
<li>Choose your own moment to withdraw. If the plane is still flying after this, even for a split second, you’ll win, just like everyone else who walked away before this point.</li>
<li>If the plane crashes when you’re still watching its flight, you’ll lose the round, along with other players who fail to leave on time.</li>
</ul>
